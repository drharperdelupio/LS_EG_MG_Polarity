Geometry and Polarity Relay Make-Up

Read this first: this is Exam 3 material

A huge portion of Exam 3 is what is covered on this page. Lewis structures, electron domains, geometry, bond angles and polarity make up a large share of the exam.

Do not just look for answers that earn the certificate. Make sure you truly understand how to answer each of these questions correctly and can do them on your own, because on the exam there are no hints and no second tries.

This individual activity covers the same skills as the Monday in-class relay (Rounds 1–2): Lewis structures, electron domains, electron geometry and bond angles, bond and molecular polarity, and molecular geometry. In class each teammate takes one role; here you complete every part for six molecules.

How it works. Work each molecule on paper first: find the valence electrons, draw the Lewis structure, then work through the rest. Enter an answer for every entry below; the Check my answers button turns on once every molecule has been fully attempted. You may try as many times as you like. After each check, the page marks the entries that need another look and tells you which step to revisit, but it does not give the answers. When you score 80% or higher, a certificate appears that you can enter your name on and print.

  • A single, double, or triple bond counts as one bonding domain.
  • Judge bond polarity from the periodic-table trend in electronegativity. You do not need electronegativity values.
  • “Focus atom” is the atom named for each molecule. Count lone pairs and domains on that atom only, except for the question about all lone pairs in the molecule.
